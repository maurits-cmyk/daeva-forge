Guaranteed or old Dragon Lord recipe?

Since the Nov 4 patch, every Star and True Dragon Lord item has two recipes. What each one really costs, failed attempts included, and which one gets you to item level 102 cheaper.

The short answer

  • Making a piece to wear? The guaranteed recipe is cheaper at every proficiency level: you never pay for a failed attempt.
  • Climbing to Splendent and beyond? Below about proficiency 75, use the guaranteed recipe and transfer-craft the Splendent piece. From around 75 to 100 (it depends on the item), the old recipe wins: 1 in 4 successes is a Splendent piece straight away, and you can sell the plain ones.
  • Crafting to sell? Only the old recipe: the guaranteed one makes Bound gear.

Star is Asmodian, True is Elyos; everything here applies to both the same way.

What changed on Nov 4

Every Star and True Dragon Lord item got a second recipe. It takes exactly the same materials and the same proficiency level as the old one, but:

  • it always succeeds,
  • it never triggers a Combo (no free Splendent version),
  • what it makes is Bound to your character.

The old recipe is unchanged: it can fail and lose the materials, but a success can Combo into the Splendent version, and the result can be traded.

All the way to item level 102

Each transfer step after the Splendent piece always succeeds, so the choice above mostly matters at the bottom of the climb. Proficiency can matter more, because the Artisan's Splendent Orichalcum pieces that Dark and Ebony need can fail too. A full Splendent Ebony Dragon Lord piece, from scratch:

  • Mace: 285M at proficiency 55, 232M at 100
  • Breastplate: 193M at 55, 190M at 100
  • Earrings: 152M at 60, 126M at 100

Your server, your items

These numbers are one server on one morning. Market prices differ a lot between servers and move every hour, so the cheapest route for you may differ.
